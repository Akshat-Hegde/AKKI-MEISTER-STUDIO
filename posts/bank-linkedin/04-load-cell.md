Why serious sim racers brake by pressure, not by pedal travel.

A potentiometer brake measures how far the pedal moves. A load-cell brake measures how hard you press. Your foot can repeat a force more reliably than a position, much like the brake in a real race car.

The main gain is consistency, lap after lap, rather than raw speed. And it only works if the rig doesn't flex under load.
