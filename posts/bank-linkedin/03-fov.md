A small setup change that makes sim driving more consistent: the correct field of view.

Horizontal FOV = 2 × arctan(W ÷ 2D), where W is the visible screen width and D is the distance from your eyes to the screen, both in the same units. At that FOV the screen behaves like a window at true scale, so speed and distance read the same every lap.

One catch: games label FOV differently (horizontal, vertical, or iRacing's 3D FOV). Check which one yours uses before entering the number.
