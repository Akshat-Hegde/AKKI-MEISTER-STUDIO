Competing at the F1 Sim Racing India Open 2026 (top 80 of 33,000+ registered) taught me one thing the hard way: battery management is race pace.

The 2026 regulations raise MGU-K power from 120 kW to 350 kW and drop the MGU-H. More electric power means where you deploy it matters more than ever.

What I'm changing: plan the deploy and harvest zones for every track before the race, and check mode and charge at the end of every straight.
