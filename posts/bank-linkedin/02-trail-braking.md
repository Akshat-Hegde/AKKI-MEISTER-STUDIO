One of the first things I work on when coaching: the brake release.

Braking moves weight onto the front tyres, which is what gives you grip to turn in. Snap off the brake at turn-in and that load moves back, so the front goes light and the car understeers.

Trail braking means keeping a little pressure as you turn in and easing it off as the steering goes on. It's not for every corner: in fast corners you'll often release earlier to keep the rear settled.
