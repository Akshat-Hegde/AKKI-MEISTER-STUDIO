# AKKI MEISTER: visual identity brief
*Motorsport & Performance · Akshat Hegde, Bengaluru*

## 01 Identity strategy
AKKI MEISTER is a driver-and-engineer brand. Akshat races sims and karts, engineered and drove a Formula Student car, reads the data, coaches drivers and builds the software around it. The identity should look like it belongs on a professional sim-racing grid next to Verstappen.com Racing, Red Bull Sim Racing and Williams Esports: dark, precise, fast and restrained. The brand earns its premium feel through discipline: one typeface family, one accent colour, one signature cut. Nothing in it is decorative for its own sake. Every element should look engineered.

**Personality:** precise · fast · composed · technical · hungry
**Positioning:** a premium personal motorsport brand for a driver who also engineers and builds.

## 02 Logo direction
- **Primary: Fusion AM monogram.** The A and M are outlined from Saira Expanded Black Italic, fused into one silhouette, and cut by a single horizontal knife slit at crossbar height. The slit is the brand's signature: an overtaking line, a data trace, the gap to the car ahead.
- **Secondary: Speed AM.** The slit is pulled out to the left as a red speed line, for headers, kit and motion.
- **Badge: Visor crest.** The fusion mark is knocked out of a chamfered visor-shaped crest, for avatars, merch and patches.
- **Wordmark:** "AKKI MEISTER" in Saira Expanded Black Italic, with "MOTORSPORT · PERFORMANCE" in Saira Regular, widely tracked.
- **Avoid:** flames, chequered-flag clip art, helmets drawn as icons, gradients on the mark, outline-only marks, more than one accent colour.
- **References:**
  - Verstappen (MV): one sharp silhouette that survives at avatar size.
  - Lando Norris (LN): italic geometric letterforms with a single idea.
  - The F1 logo: speed expressed through negative space rather than drawn speed lines.

## 03 Colour
| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Midnight | #06070B | backgrounds, about 60% |
| Depth | Deep Navy | #0C1530 | gradients, lighting, panels |
| Accent | Signal Red | #E10A23 | one accent per layout: slit, result, live |
| Type | Ice | #F4F5F7 | logo and type on dark |
| Support | Steel | #8C93A3 | labels, metadata, partner bar |

Midnight and Deep Navy form the stage; Signal Red is the voice. Red never fills more than a sixth of a frame and never sits next to a second saturated colour.

## 04 Typography
- **Display: Saira** (Google Fonts, OFL, variable in width and weight, with true italics).
  - Expanded Black Italic for the wordmark and names.
  - Condensed Black Italic for big headlines such as RACE DAY or P1.
- **Text: Inter Tight**, for body copy and captions.
- **Data: JetBrains Mono**, for lap times, deltas, timestamps and round numbers.
- **Hierarchy:** headline in Saira Condensed Black Italic, uppercase, tracking 0. Kicker in Saira Expanded Bold, uppercase, tracking 0.2em. Body in Inter Tight 400. Data in JetBrains Mono 500.
- **Avoid:** script fonts, techno or "gamer" fonts (Orbitron, Audiowide), outlined headline type.

## 05 Imagery
Cinematic and low-key. Shoot the rig, the car and the driver under hard directional light. Grade every photo to cool monochrome, with crushed blacks and a navy cast, so the only saturated colour on screen is Signal Red. Crop tight: hands on the wheel, a visor, a tyre, a data screen. No stock photos, no smiling-at-camera trophy shots without the grade.

## 06 Graphic language
- **The slit:** a horizontal knife cut, taken from the mark, that splits frames and underlines headlines.
- **Contour lines:** large, thin, low-opacity outline chevrons sitting behind the content.
- **Hairline grid:** 1px Steel rules, data labels in JetBrains Mono, and a partner bar at the bottom of every key post.

## 07 Design principles
1. **Engineered, not decorated.** Every line has a job. If it isn't information or the slit, remove it.
2. **One red thing.** A frame gets exactly one red accent, so the eye always knows where to go.
3. **Dark stage, bright subject.** Navy-black backgrounds with lit subjects, like a car under pit-lane lights.
4. **Data is the texture.** Numbers, timestamps and lap deltas replace decorative patterns.

## 08 Expressions
- **Instagram:** race-day, result, lineup and announcement posts at 4:5; countdown and on-board stories at 9:16; the crest as the avatar; mono line-icon highlight covers.
- **Website:** the wordmark top-left, a Midnight background, the slit as the section divider, data in mono.
- **Kit and merch:** the crest as a chest patch, the speed AM on the sleeve, the wordmark across the back.
- **Rig and helmet:** the speed AM along the side, the fusion mark on the chin.
- **Decks:** a Midnight title slide with the mark at 10% opacity as a watermark.
