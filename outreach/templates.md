# Outreach templates

Short, specific, value-first. **Give before you ask, ask for one small thing, and make it easy to say yes.**

**How to use them**
- Replace everything in `<angle brackets>`. If you can't fill a bracket with something specific to *them*, the message isn't ready.
- Always link **one** post that's relevant to the person you're writing to, plus the media kit (`outreach/media-kit/AKKI-MEISTER-media-kit.pdf`, or the 4 slides in a DM).
- The result line is always exactly: **"top 80 of 33,000+ registered at the F1 Sim Racing India Open 2026."**
- **No begging words:** "please", "it would mean the world", "any opportunity", "sorry to bother you", "I know you're busy". Write as a peer offering something useful.
- DMs are 3–5 lines; emails are under 150 words. If it's longer, cut it.
- Send during working hours (10 am – 6 pm IST, Tue–Thu works best for brands).

## 1. Cold DM (Instagram / Discord)

For creators, venues, organisers and anyone on IG. No attachment in the first message; the link goes in.

**Version A: you have something for them**
> Hey <name>, Akki here, India's own sim racing coach (@akki_meister).
> Loved <specific thing they did: their event / reel / league race>.
> I run The Debrief, where I review people's laps with telemetry. I'd like to <do something for them: debrief your fastest driver's lap / run a free coaching night for your regulars / feature your venue>. No cost, I just film it for the show.
> Want me to send over how it'd work?

**Version B: warm (you've raced with them)**
> Hey <name>! Akki here. I took <P3 in your ACC AM Championship / P2 at your Jeddah event> a while back.
> I've started The Debrief: weekly lap reviews with telemetry, India-first. Ep 0 is up: <link>.
> I'd love to run one at <venue>: a coaching night for your drivers, filmed for the show, your branding on it.
> Worth a quick chat this week?

**Version C: congratulations (finalists, organisers after an event)**
> Massive result at the final, <name>. <one specific moment you saw>. 🏁
> I raced the qualifiers too (top 80 of 33,000+). I'd love to put your lap on The Debrief, not to critique it but to show people what a finals lap actually looks like. Up for it?

## 4. Creator collab pitch

**DM** (works for Adi Plays; adapt the concept for each creator)

> Hey Adi, Akki here (@akki_meister). Sim racer and coach; I was top 80 of 33,000+ at the India Open this year.
> Idea for both channels: **"F1 fan vs sim racer: who's faster?"** You set a lap on my rig, I coach you for 20 minutes with the telemetry, and you go again. Your audience sees how F1 drivers actually drive; mine sees coaching work in real time.
> One reel, collab post, filmed in <city / VRH>. I'll handle the edit.
> Interested?

**Rules for creator pitches**
- The concept must be good for *their* audience first. Say what their followers get.
- Offer to do the work: the rig, the edit, the venue.
- One concrete format, not "let's collab sometime".

## 5. Follow-up

Send **one** follow-up after 5–7 working days, and a final one 2 weeks later. Then stop and log it. Every follow-up adds something new.

**Follow-up 1 (add value)**
> Hi <name>, bumping this with something new: <this week's Debrief / a relevant Apex Notes post / the Hotlap Challenge launch> → <link>. Still keen on <the small ask>. Happy to work around your timing.

**Follow-up 2 (close the loop)**
> Hi <name>, last nudge from me. If <the ask> isn't a fit right now, no problem. Who else on the team should I talk to about <creator partnerships / the sim zone>? I'll keep posting the Debrief in the meantime.

**After a "yes"**
> Brilliant, thank you. Here's what I'll send by <date>: <deliverable>. Anything you need from me before then (brand guidelines, disclosure wording, approvals)?

**After a "no" or "not now"**
> Totally understand. Thanks for getting back to me. I'll check in after <the Hotlap Challenge / next quarter> with numbers.
*(Put a date in the tracker and actually do it.)*
