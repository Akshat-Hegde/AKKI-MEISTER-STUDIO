# Outreach targets

*Built 4 Nov 2026 from `research/india-ecosystem.md`, `research/creator-landscape.md` and `strategy/STRATEGY.md`, plus a few web checks for contact routes. Pair it with `outreach/templates.md` (what to send) and `outreach/PLAN.md` (when to send it).*

**The pitch in one line:** *India's own sim racing coach. Real laps, real data, real racecraft.* Lead with **The Debrief**, and with the one number that matters: **top 80 of 33,000+ registered at the F1 Sim Racing India Open 2026.** Say it exactly like that.

**Priority**
- **P1:** contact in the next 4 weeks. Warm, or a direct fit with something you can offer now.
- **P2:** contact in month 2–3, once 4+ Debriefs and the Hotlap Challenge exist.
- **P3:** build a relationship now (follow, comment, show up), pitch later.

**Rules for every contact**
- **Public channels only:** website contact forms, published emails, official DMs, Discord, in person at events. Never personal numbers found elsewhere.
- **Small first ask.** A 15-minute call, one session, one pass. Never "sponsor me".
- **Something to look at.** Link one post that's relevant to *them*, plus the media kit PDF.
- **⚠️ = contact route not verified.** Confirm the handle or email on their official site before sending.
- Log every message in the tracker at the bottom of `PLAN.md`.

## What not to do

- Don't pitch Track Titan, MFRL or anyone else as a competitor to replace. The Indian scene is small.
- Don't round the India Open result up, and don't call yourself a "finalist". It's **top 80 of 33,000+ registered**.
- Don't promise numbers you don't have. The audience section of the media kit stays blank until it's filled with real figures.
- Don't offer exclusivity in Phase 1–2 (STRATEGY §7).
- Don't feature anyone's child in partner content without separate written parental consent.
