# 4-week outreach plan (9 Nov – 6 Dec 2026)

Runs alongside `CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`. **The rule: nobody gets a pitch until there's a live post that's relevant to them.** Each week below lists what goes live first, then who to contact.

**Budget:** about **1 hour a week** on outreach, on top of the 2 hours of community time in the strategy. Send on **Tue–Thu, 10 am – 6 pm IST**. Use the templates in `templates.md`; the targets and contact routes are in `targets.md` (numbers in brackets, e.g. [5], refer to that list).

- [ ] Fill the audience numbers in `outreach/media-kit/data.js` (follower count, avg reel views, reach, % India, top cities, age 18–34) and re-render with `node outreach/media-kit/render.js`. **Don't send the kit to brands with FILL boxes showing.** Organisers and creators are fine without numbers in weeks 1–2.
- [ ] Decide on a contact email for the kit. Then set `email` in `data.js`.
- [ ] Screenshot the India Open leaderboard (proof).
- [ ] Copy the tracker at the bottom of this file into a sheet, or keep it here.
